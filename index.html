Our Philosophy

Wine is not just
a drink — it's culture.

At Vigne & Co., we believe every glass should transport you somewhere extraordinary. Founded in the heart of Scarborough, we've spent over a decade building relationships with boutique vineyards and storied estates across five continents.

Whether you're a seasoned collector or uncorking your very first bottle, our knowledgeable staff are here to guide you — without pretension, only passion.
